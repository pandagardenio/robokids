# Week 13 · Robot Heartbeat

**Phase 2: micro:bit First Steps** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Loops and timing

## You need

- micro:bit + battery pack

## Build together

1. Load this week's program (steps below). The heart beats forever.
2. Button A = faster (excited robot). Button B = slower (sleepy robot).

## Play

Run around and count your own heartbeat, then set the robot to match it.

## Talk about it

> What does 'forever' mean for a computer?

## Level up

Add a beep on each beat.

## Load the program

Program: [`main.ts`](main.ts)

1. Click the program link, then the **Copy raw file** button (two small squares, top right of the program).
2. Go to [makecode.microbit.org](https://makecode.microbit.org), click **New Project** and name it `Week 13`.
3. Click **JavaScript** at the top, select all the text (**Ctrl+A**, Mac: **Cmd+A**) and paste (**Ctrl+V**, Mac: **Cmd+V**).
4. Click **Blocks** to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click **Download**.

First time, or something went wrong? See [Start here](../../START-HERE.md#load-a-program).

## Words this week

| Word | What it means | Say it to your kid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Timing** | How long the robot waits or does something. In programs it's often in milliseconds: 1000 = 1 second. | How long it waits. |
| **Loop** | Instructions that repeat. *Forever* repeats until the power goes off; *repeat 4* does it four times. | Do it again, and again, and again. |

Loading words like *micro:bit*, *MakeCode*, *extension* and *Blocks* are in the [Start here word list](../../START-HERE.md#word-list). All words: [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).
