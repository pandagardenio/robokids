# Week 17 · Robot Music Box

**Phase 2: micro:bit First Steps** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Sound and sequences

## You need

- micro:bit V2 (has a speaker)

## Build together

1. Load this week's program (steps below). A plays a built-in melody, B plays the kid's own tune.

## Play

Kid composes a 6-note tune with you (edit the notes in the code). Play it to the family.

## Talk about it

> What happens if you change the order of the notes?

## Level up

Make the robot play a different tune when shaken.

## Load the program

Program: [`main.ts`](main.ts)

1. Click the program link, then the **Copy raw file** button (two small squares, top right of the program).
2. Go to [makecode.microbit.org](https://makecode.microbit.org), click **New Project** and name it `Week 17`.
3. Click **JavaScript** at the top, select all the text (**Ctrl+A**, Mac: **Cmd+A**) and paste (**Ctrl+V**, Mac: **Cmd+V**).
4. Click **Blocks** to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click **Download**.

First time, or something went wrong? See [Start here](../../START-HERE.md#load-a-program).

## Words this week

| Word | What it means | Say it to your kid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sequence** | Things done in a fixed order. Change the order and you get a different result. | First this, then that. |
| **V2** | The newer micro:bit, with a speaker, a microphone and a touch-sensitive logo. This project needs it. | The newer micro:bit that can hear and talk. |
| **Code** | Another word for the program: the instructions the robot follows. | The robot's instructions. |
| **Accelerometer** | The sensor that feels movement: shaking, tilting, falling. | It feels when you shake or tip the robot. |

Loading words like *micro:bit*, *MakeCode*, *extension* and *Blocks* are in the [Start here word list](../../START-HERE.md#word-list). All words: [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).
