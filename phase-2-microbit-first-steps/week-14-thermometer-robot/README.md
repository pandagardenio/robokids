# Week 14 · Thermometer Robot

**Phase 2: micro:bit First Steps** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Temperature sensor

## You need

- micro:bit + battery pack
- Glass of cold water and warm water (in a plastic bag for the micro:bit, or just hold it near)

## Build together

1. Flash the code. Press A to see the temperature.

## Play

Temperature hunt: fridge door, sunny window, under a blanket. Kid predicts first: hotter or colder?

## Talk about it

> Where was the hottest place in the house?

## Level up

Show a snowflake below 15 °C and a sun above 25 °C.

## Code

[`main.ts`](main.ts)

Open [makecode.microbit.org](https://makecode.microbit.org) → **New Project** → switch to **JavaScript** → paste the file → **Download** and copy to the MICROBIT drive. Switch back to **Blocks** to show your kid the colorful version.
