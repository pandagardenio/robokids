# Week 9 · Two-Motor Tank

**Phase 1: Circuits and Motors** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Steering with two motors

## You need

- 2 DC motors with wheels (TT gear motors are ideal)
- 2 battery holders with switches
- Cardboard chassis
- A ball caster or a smooth bottle cap at the front

## Build together

1. Mount one motor on each side at the back.
2. Each motor gets its own battery holder and switch.
3. Both on: forward. Only left on: turns right. Only right on: turns left.

## Play

Kid drives through a cardboard maze using only the two switches.

## Talk about it

> How do you make the tank turn if there is no steering wheel?

## Level up

Reverse one motor's wires. Can you spin on the spot?
