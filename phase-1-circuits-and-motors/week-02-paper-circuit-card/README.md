# Week 2 · Paper Circuit Card

**Phase 1: Circuits and Motors** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Circuits on paper

## You need

- Copper tape (5 mm, conductive adhesive)
- 1 LED
- 1 CR2032 coin cell
- Card stock
- Binder clip

## Build together

1. Draw a robot on the card. Mark where the eye (LED) goes.
2. Run two copper tape lines from the eye to a corner (don't let them touch).
3. Bend the LED legs flat and tape them onto the lines; long leg on the + line.
4. Put the coin cell in the corner, + side up, and fold the corner over it. Clip it.

## Play

Give the glowing card to someone in the family. Can they figure out how it works?

## Talk about it

> What happens if the two copper lines touch each other?

## Level up

Add a second LED eye on the same two lines.

## Words this week

| Word | What it means | Say it to your kid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LED** | A tiny light. It only works one way round: the long leg goes to plus (+). | A tiny light that only works the right way round. |
| **Coin cell (CR2032)** | A flat, round battery the size of a big button. Dangerous if swallowed: grown-ups handle it (see [Safety](../../SAFETY.md)). | A flat battery. Only grown-ups hold it. |
| **Copper tape** | Sticky metal tape that electricity can travel along, like a flat wire. | Sticky tape that electricity can walk on. |
| **Plus (+) and minus (−)** | The two ends of a battery. Electricity leaves from one end and has to find its way back to the other. | The battery's way out and way back in. |

All words: [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).
