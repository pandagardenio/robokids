# Week 5 · Bristlebot

**Phase 1: Circuits and Motors** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Vibration makes motion

## You need

- Toothbrush head (cut off the handle)
- Mini vibration motor (coin type)
- CR2032 coin cell
- Double-sided foam tape
- Googly eyes

## Build together

1. Stick the coin cell on top of the brush head with foam tape.
2. Stick the vibration motor on the front.
3. Touch one motor wire to the bottom of the battery, tape the other wire to the top to start it.

## Play

Bristlebot races on a baking tray. Build a little maze with LEGO bricks or pencils.

## Talk about it

> The motor only shakes. How does the bot move forward?

## Level up

Bend the bristles forward or backward. Does it change direction?

## Words this week

| Word | What it means | Say it to your kid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vibration motor** | A tiny motor with a lopsided weight that makes it shake, like a phone buzzing. | A motor that shakes instead of spins. |
| **Coin cell (CR2032)** | A flat, round battery the size of a big button. Dangerous if swallowed: grown-ups handle it (see [Safety](../../SAFETY.md)). | A flat battery. Only grown-ups hold it. |
