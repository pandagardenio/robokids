🦾 Phase 4 · Smarter Robots

Week 31 Waving Robot Arm

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Servo motors

🧰 You need

  • Robot car
  • Small servo (SG90) plugged into Cutebot S1
  • Cardboard arm + hand

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Attach the cardboard arm to the servo horn.
  2. Load this week's program (steps below). A = wave hello, B = high five position.

🎮 Play Kid time

Robot greets everyone who comes home.

💬 Talk about it Together

How is a servo different from a wheel motor?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Make it wave faster when it's 'excited'.

💻 Load the program Grown-up job

Program: main.ts · needs the Cutebot extension

  1. Click the green 📋 Copy button on the program (below these steps).
  2. Go to makecode.microbit.org, click New Project and name it Week 31.
  3. Click Extensions at the bottom of the block list, search cutebot and click Cutebot.
  4. Click JavaScript at the top, select all the text (Ctrl+A, Mac: Cmd+A) and paste (Ctrl+V, Mac: Cmd+V).
  5. Click Blocks to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click Download.

First time, or something went wrong? See Start here.

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
Servo A motor that turns to an exact angle and holds still there, instead of spinning round and round. A motor that points instead of spins.
Cutebot The robot car kit this project uses. The micro:bit slots in as its brain. Our robot car.
S1 A socket on the Cutebot labeled S1 where you plug in a servo. The servo's plug spot.
Servo horn The small plastic arm that clips onto the servo and moves with it. The servo's little arm.

Loading words like micro:bit, MakeCode, extension and Blocks are in the Start here word list. All words: glossary.
