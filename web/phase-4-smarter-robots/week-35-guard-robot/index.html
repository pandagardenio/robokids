🦾 Phase 4 · Smarter Robots

Week 35 Guard Robot

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Alarms and states

🧰 You need

  • Robot car
  • A treasure (toy) to guard

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Load this week's program (steps below). Press A to arm. If something comes within 30 cm, alarm lights and sound.

🎮 Play Kid time

Sneak-up game: try to steal the treasure without triggering the guard.

💬 Talk about it Together

What does 'armed' mean for the robot?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Make the guard turn toward the thief before sounding.

💻 Load the program Grown-up job

Program: main.ts · needs the Cutebot extension

  1. Click the green 📋 Copy button on the program (below these steps).
  2. Go to makecode.microbit.org, click New Project and name it Week 35.
  3. Click Extensions at the bottom of the block list, search cutebot and click Cutebot.
  4. Click JavaScript at the top, select all the text (Ctrl+A, Mac: Cmd+A) and paste (Ctrl+V, Mac: Cmd+V).
  5. Click Blocks to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click Download.

First time, or something went wrong? See Start here.

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
State The mode the robot is in right now, like sleeping, guarding or alarm. It acts differently in each. The robot's mood right now.
Armed Switched on and watching, ready to sound the alarm. On guard.
Ultrasonic sensor The two round 'eyes' on the front of the Cutebot. They send out a sound too high to hear and time the echo to measure distance. The robot squeaks and listens for the echo, like a bat.

Loading words like micro:bit, MakeCode, extension and Blocks are in the Start here word list. All words: glossary.
