🦾 Phase 4 · Smarter Robots

Week 37 Hot and Cold Treasure Hunt

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Signal strength

🧰 You need

  • 2 micro:bits (one hidden as the 'treasure beacon')
  • Battery packs

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Load beacon.ts onto the hidden micro:bit and main.ts onto the seeker (car or handheld).

🎮 Play Kid time

Hide the beacon. The seeker's screen fills up the closer it gets. Find it!

💬 Talk about it Together

Why does the signal get stronger as you get closer?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Hide it inside a metal pot. What happens?

💻 Load the program Grown-up job

Each program goes on a different micro:bit. Load them one at a time, with that micro:bit plugged in.

Program Goes on Extension
main.ts the seeker (car or hand-held) none
beacon.ts the hidden micro:bit none
  1. Click the green 📋 Copy button on the program (below these steps).
  2. Go to makecode.microbit.org, click New Project and name it after the week and program (for example Week 37 beacon).
  3. Click JavaScript at the top, select all the text (Ctrl+A, Mac: Cmd+A) and paste (Ctrl+V, Mac: Cmd+V).
  4. Click Blocks to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click Download.

First time, or something went wrong? See Start here.

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
Signal strength How strong a radio message is when it arrives. Closer usually means stronger. Louder when close, quieter when far.
Beacon A micro:bit that keeps sending the same radio message so another one can find it. A hidden micro:bit that keeps calling 'I'm here!'.
LED screen The 25 little lights on the front of the micro:bit that show pictures, numbers and letters. The robot's face.

Loading words like micro:bit, MakeCode, extension and Blocks are in the Start here word list. All words: glossary.
