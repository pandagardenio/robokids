🖍️ Printing tips

  • Black and white is fine. Colors help, but every page works without them.
  • Print the activity book in parts: in the print dialog, choose Pages. Each week is two pages.
  • Journal pages look great hole-punched in a binder: by week 50 your kid has a whole robot book.
  • Turn on Background graphics in the print dialog for the colored headers.
