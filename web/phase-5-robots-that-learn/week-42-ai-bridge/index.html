🧠 Phase 5 · Robots That Learn

Week 42 The AI Bridge

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Connecting AI to a robot

🧰 You need

  • Laptop + Chrome/Edge
  • micro:bit + USB cable
  • Last week's model (Export → Upload → copy the link)

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Load main.ts onto the micro:bit and keep it plugged in by USB.
  2. Close MakeCode, open the AI bridge, paste the model link, click Connect micro:bit, then Start AI (full steps).
  3. The micro:bit shows ✓ or ✗ depending on your thumb.

🎮 Play Kid time

Silent game: answer yes/no questions with thumbs only; the robot shows the answer.

💬 Talk about it Together

Who decides: the AI or the micro:bit? (Both: AI sees, micro:bit acts.)

⭐ Level up Kid time

Make a happy and sad face instead of ✓ and ✗.

💻 Load the program Grown-up job

Program: main.ts

  1. Click the green 📋 Copy button on the program (below these steps).
  2. Go to makecode.microbit.org, click New Project and name it Week 42.
  3. Click JavaScript at the top, select all the text (Ctrl+A, Mac: Cmd+A) and paste (Ctrl+V, Mac: Cmd+V).
  4. Click Blocks to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click Download.

First time, or something went wrong? See Start here.

Then open the AI bridge in Chrome or Edge and follow the AI bridge guide. Close MakeCode first.

In Teachable Machine, name the classes exactly like this, lowercase: thumbsup, thumbsdown (plus nothing).

Keep this micro:bit plugged into the laptop with its USB cable.

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
AI Artificial intelligence: a computer program that learns from examples instead of following rules someone wrote. A computer that learns by looking at examples.
AI bridge A page from this project that passes what the AI sees or hears to the micro:bit. See the AI bridge guide. The messenger between the AI and the robot.
USB The cable between the laptop and the micro:bit. It carries messages, not just power. The cable that lets the laptop talk to the robot.
Model What the AI ends up with after training: its 'memory' of what it learned. Exporting it gives you a link. The AI's memory.
Export In Teachable Machine: saving the trained model online so you get a link to paste into the AI bridge. Sending the AI's memory to the internet so the robot can use it.

Loading words like micro:bit, MakeCode, extension and Blocks are in the Start here word list. All words: glossary.
