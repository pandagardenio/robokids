🧠 Phase 5 · Robots That Learn

Week 46 The Bad Data Experiment

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Data quality

🧰 You need

  • Week 45 setup

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Train a 'bad' model: only 3 photos per class, in the dark, all from one angle.
  2. Train a 'good' model: 50+ photos, lights on, many angles.
  3. Test both with the same toys.

🎮 Play Kid time

Robot quiz: which model wins more rounds? Kid keeps score.

💬 Talk about it Together

Why did the bad robot get confused?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Train with only teddies wearing hats. Then show a teddy without a hat.

💻 Load the program Grown-up job

Program: main.ts

  1. Click the green 📋 Copy button on the program (below these steps).
  2. Go to makecode.microbit.org, click New Project and name it Week 46.
  3. Click JavaScript at the top, select all the text (Ctrl+A, Mac: Cmd+A) and paste (Ctrl+V, Mac: Cmd+V).
  4. Click Blocks to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click Download.

First time, or something went wrong? See Start here.

Then open the AI bridge in Chrome or Edge and follow the AI bridge guide. Close MakeCode first.

In Teachable Machine, name the classes exactly like this, lowercase: teddy, car, ball (plus nothing).

Keep this micro:bit plugged into the laptop with its USB cable.

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
Training Showing the AI lots of examples so it learns to tell things apart. Teaching the AI by showing it.
Model What the AI ends up with after training: its 'memory' of what it learned. Exporting it gives you a link. The AI's memory.
Examples / data The photos or sounds you show the AI. More, varied examples make a smarter AI; few or similar ones make a confused one. The pictures we show the AI to learn from.
Class One of the things the AI learns to tell apart, like thumbsup or teddy. Names must match the week's page exactly. One thing the AI learns to spot.
