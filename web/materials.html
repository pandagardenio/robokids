Materials

Buy each phase's items when you get there. From week 11 you also need a laptop (Windows, Mac, Linux or Chromebook) with Chrome or Edge; see Start here. Prices are rough estimates in euros. Links go to the official manufacturer store or reseller finder; generic parts (LEDs, tape, batteries, SG90 servo) are sold by any electronics shop.

Phase Items Approx. cost
1 · Circuits and Motors 5 mm LEDs (pack of 50), CR2032 coin cells, conductive copper tape, 2×AA battery holders with switch, alligator clip leads, coin vibration motors, small DC motors (3–6 V), 2 TT gear motors with wheels, propellers, toothbrush heads, paper cups, markers, cardboard ~30 €
2 · micro:bit First Steps 2× BBC micro:bit V2 Go kit (micro:bit V2 + USB cable + 2×AAA battery pack; needs V2 for speaker, microphone and touch logo). The second one is only needed from week 19 (see note below) 25–50 €
3 · The Robot Car Elecfreaks Cutebot (includes ultrasonic sensor, line sensors, RGB headlights, servo ports), 3×AAA batteries, black electrical tape ~45 €
4 · Smarter Robots SG90 micro servo, felt-tip markers, foam/Duplo blocks ~5 €
5 · Robots That Learn Laptop with webcam and microphone (the same laptop as before). Teachable Machine is free 0 €

Shopping shortcut: two micro:bit V2 "Go" kits, a Cutebot and a basic electronics starter pack cover the whole year.

Why two micro:bits? In these weeks one micro:bit talks to the other by radio, so you need both:

Week What the second micro:bit does
19 · Secret Radio Sends and receives messages
28 · Tilt Remote Hand-held remote for the car
37 · Hot/Cold Treasure Hidden beacon
39 · Robot Tag The tagger
43 · Gesture Car Relay between the laptop and the car
44 · Clap and Whistle AI Relay plugged into the laptop, same as week 43

Every other week needs just one, so you can buy the second kit later, around week 19.

Other car kit: DFRobot Maqueen also works. The programs are written for the Cutebot, so ask your AI assistant to adapt each car program: paste the program and say "Change this MakeCode program so it works with a DFRobot Maqueen instead of a Cutebot."

Want to go deeper? The Elecfreaks Cutebot Pro is a pricier upgrade with encoder motors for precise distances and turns, and 4 line sensors instead of 2. It uses its own Cutebot Pro extension, so the programs need adapting the same way.
