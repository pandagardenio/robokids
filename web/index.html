A Year of Robots

Build, play and tinker together. You get things ready; your kid builds, names, tests and makes it their own. No coding experience needed.

🚀 Start week 1 👋 Grown-ups: start here 🖨️ Printables

Tick off a week when it's done and watch your robot year fill up!

🧭 How it works

  • One build session per week (30–45 min), plus one or two 10–15 minute play sessions.
  • Every week has the same shape: Build → Play → Talk about it → Level up → Robot Journal.
  • Kid leads, parent assists. They choose names, colors, rules and numbers. You handle batteries, wiring and loading the programs.
  • Weeks 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 are showcases with slack built in. Skip or repeat any week.
  • Track progress with the progress checklist.

Weeks 1–10 need no laptop at all: just LEDs, batteries and motors.
