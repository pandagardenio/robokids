📟 Phase 2 · micro:bit First Steps

Week 11 Robot Pet Face

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Inputs and outputs

🧰 You need

  • BBC micro:bit V2 + USB cable
  • Battery pack (2×AAA)
  • Cardboard pet body

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Plug in the micro:bit and load this week's program (steps below).
  2. Build a cardboard pet with a window for the LED screen.

🎮 Play Kid time

Button A = happy, B = sad, A+B = surprised, shake = dizzy. Kid decides what each one means for the pet.

💬 Talk about it Together

What are the pet's inputs? What are its outputs?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Change the icons. Draw your own face with the LED editor.

💻 Load the program Grown-up job

Program: main.ts

  1. Click the green 📋 Copy button on the program (below these steps).
  2. Go to makecode.microbit.org, click New Project and name it Week 11.
  3. Click JavaScript at the top, select all the text (Ctrl+A, Mac: Cmd+A) and paste (Ctrl+V, Mac: Cmd+V).
  4. Click Blocks to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click Download.

First time, or something went wrong? See Start here.

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
Input Something that goes into the robot: a button press, a shake, light, sound. What the robot feels.
Output Something the robot does: lights, sounds, moving. What the robot does.
V2 The newer micro:bit, with a speaker, a microphone and a touch-sensitive logo. This project needs it. The newer micro:bit that can hear and talk.
USB The cable between the laptop and the micro:bit. It carries messages, not just power. The cable that lets the laptop talk to the robot.
LED screen The 25 little lights on the front of the micro:bit that show pictures, numbers and letters. The robot's face.
Accelerometer The sensor that feels movement: shaking, tilting, falling. It feels when you shake or tip the robot.
Icon A small picture shown on the LED screen, like a heart or a smiley. A tiny picture made of lights.

Loading words like micro:bit, MakeCode, extension and Blocks are in the Start here word list. All words: glossary.
