🚗 Phase 3 · The Robot Car

Week 28 Tilt Remote Control

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Radio + accelerometer

🧰 You need

  • Robot car
  • Second micro:bit as remote

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Load remote.ts onto the hand micro:bit and main.ts onto the car.
  2. Tilt forward to drive, tilt sideways to turn, press A to stop.

🎮 Play Kid time

Treasure hunt: drive the car to pick up paper 'treasure' with a cardboard scoop.

💬 Talk about it Together

How does tilting your hand move the car?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Press B to honk.

💻 Load the program Grown-up job

Each program goes on a different micro:bit. Load them one at a time, with that micro:bit plugged in.

Program Goes on Extension
main.ts the car Cutebot
remote.ts the hand-held remote none
  1. Click the green 📋 Copy button on the program (below these steps).
  2. Go to makecode.microbit.org, click New Project and name it after the week and program (for example Week 28 remote).
  3. Click Extensions at the bottom of the block list, search cutebot and click Cutebot (only for programs that need it).
  4. Click JavaScript at the top, select all the text (Ctrl+A, Mac: Cmd+A) and paste (Ctrl+V, Mac: Cmd+V).
  5. Click Blocks to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click Download.

First time, or something went wrong? See Start here.

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
Radio micro:bits can send short messages to each other through the air, no wires needed, across a house. Invisible messages through the air.
Accelerometer The sensor that feels movement: shaking, tilting, falling. It feels when you shake or tip the robot.

Loading words like micro:bit, MakeCode, extension and Blocks are in the Start here word list. All words: glossary.
