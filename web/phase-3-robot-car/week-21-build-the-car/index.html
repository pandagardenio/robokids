🚗 Phase 3 · The Robot Car

Week 21 Build the Robot Car

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Parts of a robot

🧰 You need

  • Elecfreaks Cutebot kit (or similar micro:bit car)
  • micro:bit
  • 3×AAA batteries (in the kit's holder)
  • Small screwdriver

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Unbox together and name every part: wheels, motors, sensors, lights, brain (micro:bit).
  2. Insert batteries (parent), slot the micro:bit in, screen facing forward.
  3. Load main.ts: the car says hello, lights up, and wiggles.

🎮 Play Kid time

Name the robot and make a cardboard name plate.

💬 Talk about it Together

Which part is the robot's brain? Its eyes? Its legs?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Draw the robot in the Robot Journal and label the parts.

💻 Load the program Grown-up job

Program: main.ts · needs the Cutebot extension

  1. Click the green 📋 Copy button on the program (below these steps).
  2. Go to makecode.microbit.org, click New Project and name it Week 21.
  3. Click Extensions at the bottom of the block list, search cutebot and click Cutebot.
  4. Click JavaScript at the top, select all the text (Ctrl+A, Mac: Cmd+A) and paste (Ctrl+V, Mac: Cmd+V).
  5. Click Blocks to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click Download.

First time, or something went wrong? See Start here.

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
Cutebot The robot car kit this project uses. The micro:bit slots in as its brain. Our robot car.
Sensor A part that lets the robot notice the world: light, heat, distance, sound, movement. The robot's eyes, ears and skin.

Loading words like micro:bit, MakeCode, extension and Blocks are in the Start here word list. All words: glossary.
