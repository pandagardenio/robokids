💡 Phase 1 · Circuits and Motors

Week 3 The Robot Switch

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Open and closed circuits

🧰 You need

  • Last week's card
  • Extra copper tape
  • Paper fastener or pushbutton (optional)

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Cut one copper line so there is a gap.
  2. Make a paper flap with copper tape underneath that covers the gap when pressed.
  3. Press the flap: light on. Let go: light off.

🎮 Play Kid time

'Wink game': you call out 'wink!' and they press. Then switch roles and try Morse-style blink patterns (short, long).

💬 Talk about it Together

Where else at home do you see switches?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Make a switch that turns on when the card is opened, like a magic greeting card.

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
Switch Something that opens or closes a gap in a circuit, to turn things off and on. A little door for electricity: open means off, closed means on.
Circuit A complete loop that electricity travels around: out of the battery, through the parts, and back in. Any gap stops everything. A round trip for electricity. If the path is broken, nothing works.
Copper tape Sticky metal tape that electricity can travel along, like a flat wire. Sticky tape that electricity can walk on.

All words: glossary.
