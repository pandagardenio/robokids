💡 Phase 1 · Circuits and Motors

Week 6 Scribble Bot

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Unbalanced motors

🧰 You need

  • Paper cup
  • Small DC motor (3–6 V)
  • AA battery holder + 2 AA
  • 3 felt-tip markers
  • Hot glue (parent)
  • Cork or eraser piece

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Tape the markers around the cup as legs, caps off.
  2. Glue the battery holder on top and the motor on the side.
  3. Push a cork piece off-center onto the motor shaft so it wobbles.
  4. Place on big paper and switch on.

🎮 Play Kid time

Make robot art. Frame the best one in the Robot Journal.

💬 Talk about it Together

Why does putting the cork off-center make it shake?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Move the cork to the center. What changes in the drawing?

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
Off-center Weight that isn't in the middle of something spinning, which makes it wobble and shake. Lopsided, so it wobbles.
DC motor A motor that spins when connected to a battery. Swap its two wires and it spins the other way. A spinner that runs on batteries.
Volts (V) How hard a battery pushes electricity. One AA battery is 1.5 V; two make 3 V. How strong the battery's push is.
Shaft The metal rod that sticks out of a motor and spins. The motor's spinning nose.

All words: glossary.
