💡 Phase 1 · Circuits and Motors

Week 7 Wobble-Bot Sumo

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Weight and balance

🧰 You need

  • 2 scribble bots or bristlebots
  • A shoebox lid as the ring
  • Modeling clay

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Add clay to make each bot heavier or lower.
  2. Draw a circle in the lid as the sumo ring.

🎮 Play Kid time

Sumo: both bots start in the ring, last one inside wins. Best of five.

💬 Talk about it Together

Did the heavier robot win more?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Design a bumper that pushes better (cardboard plow, rubber band).

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
Balance How weight is spread out. Low and heavy robots are harder to tip over. Low and heavy is hard to push over.

All words: glossary.
