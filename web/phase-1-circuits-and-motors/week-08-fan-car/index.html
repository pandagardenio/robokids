💡 Phase 1 · Circuits and Motors

Week 8 Fan Car

⏱️ ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week 🎯 Skill: Push and measuring

🧰 You need

  • Cardboard chassis
  • 4 bottle caps + 2 skewers + straws (axles)
  • DC motor + propeller
  • AA battery holder with switch

🔧 Build together Together

  1. Straws taped under the chassis, skewers through, bottle caps as wheels.
  2. Mount the motor at the back facing backward with the propeller.
  3. Switch on and let it go.

🎮 Play Kid time

Distance contest: mark the floor with tape every 50 cm. Who can tweak the car to go furthest?

💬 Talk about it Together

Which way does the air go, and which way does the car go?

⭐ Level up Kid time

Flip the motor wires. What happens?

📖 Words this week For grown-ups

Word What it means Say it to your kid
Chassis The base of a vehicle that everything else is attached to. The robot's skeleton.
Axle The rod that wheels turn on. The stick the wheels spin on.
DC motor A motor that spins when connected to a battery. Swap its two wires and it spins the other way. A spinner that runs on batteries.
Propeller Spinning blades that push air one way, so the vehicle moves the other way. Spinning blades that push air.
Switch Something that opens or closes a gap in a circuit, to turn things off and on. A little door for electricity: open means off, closed means on.

All words: glossary.
