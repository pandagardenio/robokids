# Week 32 · Bulldozer

**Phase 4: Smarter Robots** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Pushing and power

## You need

- Robot car
- Cardboard plow taped to the front
- Duplo or foam blocks

## Build together

1. Load this week's program (steps below). The bulldozer pushes forward until it sees a wall, then backs up.

## Play

Clean-up time: push blocks into a 'garage' (shoebox on its side).

## Talk about it

> Is the robot faster pushing or not pushing? Why?

## Level up

Use the servo arm as a scoop.

## Load the program

Program: [`main.ts`](main.ts) · needs the **Cutebot** extension

1. Click the program link, then the **Copy raw file** button (two small squares, top right of the program).
2. Go to [makecode.microbit.org](https://makecode.microbit.org), click **New Project** and name it `Week 32`.
3. Click **Extensions** at the bottom of the block list, search `cutebot` and click **Cutebot**.
4. Click **JavaScript** at the top, select all the text (**Ctrl+A**, Mac: **Cmd+A**) and paste (**Ctrl+V**, Mac: **Cmd+V**).
5. Click **Blocks** to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click **Download**.

First time, or something went wrong? See [Start here](../../START-HERE.md#load-a-program).
