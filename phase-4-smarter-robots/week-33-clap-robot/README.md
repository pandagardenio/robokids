# Week 33 · Clap-Activated Robot

**Phase 4: Smarter Robots** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Sound sensor

## You need

- Robot car with micro:bit V2 (microphone)

## Build together

1. Load this week's program (steps below). One loud clap = go / stop. The LED shows how loud the room is.

## Play

Freeze dance with the robot: clap to start and stop.

## Talk about it

> Does the robot hear words or only loudness?

## Level up

Two claps = spin (count claps in a short time window).

## Load the program

Program: [`main.ts`](main.ts) · needs the **Cutebot** extension

1. Click the program link, then the **Copy raw file** button (two small squares, top right of the program).
2. Go to [makecode.microbit.org](https://makecode.microbit.org), click **New Project** and name it `Week 33`.
3. Click **Extensions** at the bottom of the block list, search `cutebot` and click **Cutebot**.
4. Click **JavaScript** at the top, select all the text (**Ctrl+A**, Mac: **Cmd+A**) and paste (**Ctrl+V**, Mac: **Cmd+V**).
5. Click **Blocks** to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click **Download**.

First time, or something went wrong? See [Start here](../../START-HERE.md#load-a-program).

## Words this week

| Word | What it means | Say it to your kid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Microphone** | The micro:bit V2 can measure how loud it is. It hears loudness, not words. | The robot's ear. It hears loud and quiet, not words. |
| **Sensor** | A part that lets the robot notice the world: light, heat, distance, sound, movement. | The robot's eyes, ears and skin. |
| **V2** | The newer micro:bit, with a speaker, a microphone and a touch-sensitive logo. This project needs it. | The newer micro:bit that can hear and talk. |
| **LED** | A tiny light. It only works one way round: the long leg goes to plus (+). | A tiny light that only works the right way round. |

Loading words like *micro:bit*, *MakeCode*, *extension* and *Blocks* are in the [Start here word list](../../START-HERE.md#word-list). All words: [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).
