# Week 38 · Drawing Robot

**Phase 4: Smarter Robots** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Geometry with code

## You need

- Robot car
- Felt-tip marker taped to the back center
- Large paper

## Build together

1. Load this week's program (steps below). A = square, B = triangle, A+B = flower (loop of loops).

## Play

Make an art gallery of robot drawings.

## Talk about it

> How many turns make a full circle?

## Level up

Write their initial with the robot.

## Load the program

Program: [`main.ts`](main.ts) · needs the **Cutebot** extension

1. Click the program link, then the **Copy raw file** button (two small squares, top right of the program).
2. Go to [makecode.microbit.org](https://makecode.microbit.org), click **New Project** and name it `Week 38`.
3. Click **Extensions** at the bottom of the block list, search `cutebot` and click **Cutebot**.
4. Click **JavaScript** at the top, select all the text (**Ctrl+A**, Mac: **Cmd+A**) and paste (**Ctrl+V**, Mac: **Cmd+V**).
5. Click **Blocks** to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click **Download**.

First time, or something went wrong? See [Start here](../../START-HERE.md#load-a-program).

## Words this week

| Word | What it means | Say it to your kid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Geometry** | The maths of shapes. A full turn is 360°; a square has four turns of 90°. | Shapes and turns. |
| **Loop** | Instructions that repeat. *Forever* repeats until the power goes off; *repeat 4* does it four times. | Do it again, and again, and again. |

Loading words like *micro:bit*, *MakeCode*, *extension* and *Blocks* are in the [Start here word list](../../START-HERE.md#word-list). All words: [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).
