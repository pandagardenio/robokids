# Materials

Buy each phase's items when you get there. Prices are rough estimates in euros.

| Phase | Items | Approx. cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Circuits and Motors | 5 mm LEDs (pack of 50), CR2032 coin cells, conductive copper tape, 2×AA battery holders with switch, alligator clip leads, coin vibration motors, small DC motors (3–6 V), 2 TT gear motors with wheels, propellers, toothbrush heads, paper cups, markers, cardboard | ~30 € |
| 2 · micro:bit First Steps | BBC micro:bit **V2** (needs V2 for speaker, microphone and touch logo) + USB cable + 2×AAA battery pack. A second micro:bit for weeks 19, 28, 37, 39, 43–44 | 25–50 € |
| 3 · The Robot Car | Elecfreaks Cutebot (includes ultrasonic sensor, line sensors, RGB headlights, servo ports), 3×AAA batteries, black electrical tape | ~45 € |
| 4 · Smarter Robots | SG90 micro servo, felt-tip markers, foam/Duplo blocks | ~5 € |
| 5 · Robots That Learn | Laptop with webcam and microphone, Chrome or Edge browser. [Teachable Machine](https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com) is free | 0 € |

**Shopping shortcut:** a micro:bit V2 "Go" kit, a second bare micro:bit, a Cutebot and a basic electronics starter pack cover the whole year.

**Other car kits:** Kitronik Move:mini, DFRobot Maqueen or Elecfreaks Cutebot Pro all work. Rewrite the kit adapter at the top of each car file; line and distance sensor calls may differ.
