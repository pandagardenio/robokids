# Materials

Buy each phase's items when you get there. Prices are rough estimates in euros. Links go to the official manufacturer store or reseller finder; generic parts (LEDs, tape, batteries, SG90 servo) are sold by any electronics shop.

| Phase | Items | Approx. cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Circuits and Motors | 5 mm LEDs (pack of 50), CR2032 coin cells, conductive copper tape, 2×AA battery holders with switch, alligator clip leads, coin vibration motors, small DC motors (3–6 V), 2 TT gear motors with wheels, propellers, toothbrush heads, paper cups, markers, cardboard | ~30 € |
| 2 · micro:bit First Steps | [BBC micro:bit **V2**](https://microbit.org/buy/) (needs V2 for speaker, microphone and touch logo) + USB cable + 2×AAA battery pack. A second micro:bit for weeks 19, 28, 37, 39, 43–44 | 25–50 € |
| 3 · The Robot Car | [Elecfreaks Cutebot](https://shop.elecfreaks.com/products/elecfreaks-micro-bit-smart-cutebot-kit-without-micro-bit-board) (includes ultrasonic sensor, line sensors, RGB headlights, servo ports), 3×AAA batteries, black electrical tape | ~45 € |
| 4 · Smarter Robots | SG90 micro servo, felt-tip markers, foam/Duplo blocks | ~5 € |
| 5 · Robots That Learn | Laptop with webcam and microphone, Chrome or Edge browser. [Teachable Machine](https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com) is free | 0 € |

**Shopping shortcut:** a micro:bit V2 "Go" kit and a second bare micro:bit (both from an [official reseller](https://microbit.org/buy/)), a [Cutebot](https://shop.elecfreaks.com/products/elecfreaks-micro-bit-smart-cutebot-kit-without-micro-bit-board) and a basic electronics starter pack cover the whole year.

**Other car kit:** [DFRobot Maqueen](https://www.dfrobot.com/product-1783.html) also works. Rewrite the kit adapter at the top of each car file; line and distance sensor calls may differ.

**Want to go deeper?** The [Elecfreaks Cutebot Pro](https://shop.elecfreaks.com/search?q=cutebot+pro) is a pricier upgrade with encoder motors for precise distances and turns, and 4 line sensors instead of 2. It uses its own `cutebot-pro` extension, so rewrite the kit adapter the same way.
