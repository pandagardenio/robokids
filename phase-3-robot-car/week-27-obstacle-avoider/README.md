# Week 27 · Obstacle Avoider

**Phase 3: The Robot Car** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Distance sensor

## You need

- Robot car with ultrasonic sensor (included with Cutebot)
- Boxes and books as walls

## Build together

1. Load this week's program (steps below). The robot drives until something is closer than 15 cm, then backs up and turns.

## Play

Escape the cave: build a box maze and see how long it takes to get out.

## Talk about it

> How does the robot 'see' without eyes? (Like a bat.)

## Level up

Change 15 cm to 30 cm. Is it more careful now?

## Load the program

Program: [`main.ts`](main.ts) · needs the **Cutebot** extension

1. Click the program link, then the **Copy raw file** button (two small squares, top right of the program).
2. Go to [makecode.microbit.org](https://makecode.microbit.org), click **New Project** and name it `Week 27`.
3. Click **Extensions** at the bottom of the block list, search `cutebot` and click **Cutebot**.
4. Click **JavaScript** at the top, select all the text (**Ctrl+A**, Mac: **Cmd+A**) and paste (**Ctrl+V**, Mac: **Cmd+V**).
5. Click **Blocks** to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click **Download**.

First time, or something went wrong? See [Start here](../../START-HERE.md#load-a-program).
