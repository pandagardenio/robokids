# Week 26 · Line Follower

**Phase 3: The Robot Car** · ~45 min build + short play sessions during the week · **Skill:** Sensors + if/else

## You need

- Robot car
- Black electrical tape (2 cm wide)
- Big white paper or light floor

## Build together

1. Make a closed loop track with gentle curves.
2. Load this week's program (steps below). Put the robot on the line and press A.

## Play

Kid designs new tracks each day. Add a 'station' where the robot should stop (you can add that in code).

## Talk about it

> How does the robot know it's leaving the line?

## Level up

Make a figure-8 track. Does it still work?

## Load the program

Program: [`main.ts`](main.ts) · needs the **Cutebot** extension

1. Click the program link, then the **Copy raw file** button (two small squares, top right of the program).
2. Go to [makecode.microbit.org](https://makecode.microbit.org), click **New Project** and name it `Week 26`.
3. Click **Extensions** at the bottom of the block list, search `cutebot` and click **Cutebot**.
4. Click **JavaScript** at the top, select all the text (**Ctrl+A**, Mac: **Cmd+A**) and paste (**Ctrl+V**, Mac: **Cmd+V**).
5. Click **Blocks** to see the colorful version your kid can change, then click **Download**.

First time, or something went wrong? See [Start here](../../START-HERE.md#load-a-program).

## Words this week

| Word | What it means | Say it to your kid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **If / else** | The program checks something: IF it's true, do one thing, ELSE (otherwise) do another. | If it rains, take an umbrella, else take sunglasses. |
| **Line sensor** | Two small sensors under the car that see whether the floor below is dark or light. | The robot's eyes looking at the floor. |
| **Code** | Another word for the program: the instructions the robot follows. | The robot's instructions. |

Loading words like *micro:bit*, *MakeCode*, *extension* and *Blocks* are in the [Start here word list](../../START-HERE.md#word-list). All words: [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md).
